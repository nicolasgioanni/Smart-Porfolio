## Inline diagram explainers

The Adversarial Machine Learning and GuideDonorScheduler explainers are portfolio UI diagrams, not source assets or experimental results. They use checked-in inline SVG and CSS only; they introduce no remote media, fetch, dependency, or publication-rights claim. Each connected scene exposes its complete caption and persistent HTML workflow labels before and during its optional twelve-second animation.

The Adversarial Machine Learning scene is an illustrative detector-training experiment: clean and manipulated `7` cards feed visible paths into a circular binary detector, then a clean test card reaches the neutral question `Clean or manipulated?`. Sample travel, changed-pixel emphasis, and detector-ring rotation communicate the connected workflow without changing its claim. Its caption is: “An illustrative experiment: train a detector using clean and manipulated digit images.” The published source evidence is the pinned [`Detector.py`](https://github.com/nicolasgioanni/Independent-Study/blob/5c8dfe6b94a7ad3a19e6a0f16007306415a971ae/Nicolas%20Gioanni/Defenses/Detector.py): it creates MNIST and FGM examples for detector training, then runs its final check on a clean `x_test` input. The diagram must not imply a guaranteed detection result, a corrected class, or measured performance that the script does not establish.

The GuideDonorScheduler scene represents `target DNA + requested A→G change → guide → donor with requested and optional silent PAM/seed changes → guide + donor spreadsheet design output`. A target-DNA ribbon, nearby guide bracket, aligned donor ribbon, and guide/donor rows moving into a spreadsheet make the sequence design connection explicit. Its source evidence is the pinned [GuideDonorScheduler README](https://github.com/BrentLagesse/GuideDonorScheduler/blob/01231e21616e1ee4eb205f5a45734b0a7385997f/README.md). Present it as schematic sequence design: do not show DNA being replaced by RNA or claim a successful laboratory result.

The diagrams cycle four three-second emphasis phases only while eligible to run: the first three seconds introduce the requested source, the next three trace guide or training inputs, the next three show the donor or detector, and seconds nine through eleven show the spreadsheet or neutral check before a one-second complete-scene hold. Their native `Pause` and `Resume` control is at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels. Offscreen and hidden documents suspend activity without changing the `Pause` action; a manual pause changes it to `Resume` and remains paused when the diagram becomes eligible again. Initial reduced-motion or unsupported-observer rendering keeps the full static diagram and caption visible and omits the control. If reduced motion is enabled while that control has focus, the disabled static control remains only until focus leaves so focus is not lost.
